BeCoffee Privacy Notice

National Privacy Commission (NPC) Circular & Data Subject Rights

Effective Date: January 2026 · Operating Entity: BeCoffee Roasters Inc. (Zamboanga City, Philippines)

1. General Principle & Commitment

In adherence to Republic Act No. 10173, otherwise known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (DPA), its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR), and issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC), BeCoffee is dedicated to safeguarding your personal data privacy and ensuring transparency in all our processing activities.

2. Personal Data We Collect

  • Member Accounts: Full name, verified email address, Philippine mobile contact number (+63), securely hashed password.
  • Reservations & Cupping Experiences: Preferred outpost (Putik Flagship or Baliwasan Roastery), party size, reservation date & time slot, dietary notes or special requests.
  • Orders & Transacting: Drink specifications (temperature, size, dairy preferences), delivery/pickup timeline, transaction totals in Philippine Pesos (₱).

3. Purpose of Processing

We collect and utilize your personal information exclusively for:

  • Facilitating specialty coffee pickup orders, brew workshop scheduling, and slow bar reservations.
  • Managing your authenticated member account credentials with bcrypt protection.
  • Direct transactional alerts (SMS/Email confirmations regarding your table bookings or fresh bean orders).
  • Internal roastery operations and fulfilling local Philippine tax/regulatory obligations.

4. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you possess the following statutory rights:

  • Right to be Informed: To understand how and why your personal data is collected and processed.
  • Right to Access: To request a copy of your personal record stored within our systems.
  • Right to Rectification: To modify your name, contact phone, or password at any time via Account Settings.
  • Right to Erasure or Blocking: To request account deletion and removal from reservation archives.
  • Right to File a Complaint: Before the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph) should you feel your rights have been violated.

5. Data Storage & Security Measures

Your records are retained on secure servers with strict access boundaries, parameterized PDO statements, HTTPS encryption, and session isolation. We never sell or lease your personal information to third-party advertisers or brokers.

6. Contact Our Data Protection Officer

For inquiries regarding your personal data rights, email our Data Protection Officer at privacy@becoffee.ph or visit our Flagship Sanctuary: RCDAO Village, Putik, Zamboanga City, 7000, Philippines.
